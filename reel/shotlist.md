# Truffle history reel: shot list

Format: 1080x1920, 30 fps, no captions. Each shot's length will be set from the voiceover's word timing.

Transitions copied from the inspo reel:
- **zoom-blur**: a quick, blurred push of about 0.2 s. This is the default between photos.
- **whip**: a sideways slide with motion blur, used between two related photos.
- **burst**: a radial zoom-blur burst that opens a new section.
- **cut**: a hard cut on a word, used inside a section.

Movement on each photo is either a slow push-in (`push`) or a slow pan across a wide image (`pan`).
