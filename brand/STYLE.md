# American Heritage Chocolate – reel style guide

Separate client from VEXON. Do not reuse the VEXON look (orange boxes, slam titles, flashes, RGB split, outlined numbers, heavy grade).

Based on the brand sheet plus their Instagram grid and two reference reels
(instagram.com/p/DfqI-nlBhRP – Pumpkin Spice hot chocolate, instagram.com/p/DfIJVA9uY0T – Chocolate Bread Pudding).

## Colours
- Brown `#592d2b`: text stroke, text boxes, end card.
- Black `#000000`: alternative caption stroke.
- White: text fill.
- Cream paper (~`#f3ede3`, light paper texture): "Did you know?" style cards with a thin brown swirl line.

## Text treatments (as used on their feed)
1. **Main on-screen text** (most reels): white Montserrat Bold, thin brown `#592d2b` stroke,
   sentence case, centred, 2–4 short lines, upper third of frame (~y 400–700 on 1080×1920).
   Product name written as "AMERICAN HERITAGE®" in caps.
2. **Boxed text** (story/fact hooks): white Montserrat SemiBold on a solid brown box with a
   thin lighter inset border, upper third.
3. **Italic accent:** brown italic serif ("Did you know?") – True Fate in CapCut. Use sparingly.
4. **Closed captions:** Montserrat ALL CAPS, black or brown stroke.
5. Small logo watermark bottom-centre on graphic cards.

## Look
- Natural, bright home-kitchen footage. Warm it slightly; no heavy grade, no grain, no film effects.
- Real hands and real people (creator talking/sipping to camera) are part of the brand voice.
- Simple cuts; text appears cleanly (fade/soft pop), no flashy transitions.

## Copy tone
Warm, cozy, a little playful, rooted in history ("from an authentic 1700's recipe").
Captions end with a soft CTA + "[Links in bio] 🍫" and hashtags like
#AmericanHeritageChocolate #HeritageBaking #HolidayBaking.
